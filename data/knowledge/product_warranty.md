# Product Warranty and Defect Support
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. The warranty terms below are fictional sample terms.

## Demo warranty period
For this demo, eligible physical products have a limited 12-month warranty from the delivery date against manufacturing defects. This example does not cover every product type, and the actual eligibility must be checked against the relevant product record.

## Potentially covered defects
A manufacturing defect that appears during normal intended use may qualify for repair, replacement, or another remedy after review. The assistant must not decide technical fault solely from a short chat description.

## Exclusions and uncertainty
Accidental damage, unauthorized modification, normal wear, and misuse may be excluded under this fictional policy. If the cause is unclear, avoid rejecting the claim; route it for technical review.

## Warranty claim information
Collect the order reference, product model, delivery date, and a description of the issue. Request photos only through an approved secure channel. Do not ask the customer to disassemble equipment or perform unsafe repairs.

## Out-of-warranty requests
For items outside the demo warranty period, explain that standard warranty coverage may not apply and offer a paid-service inquiry or human review where available. Do not promise free service.
