# Billing, Invoices, and Charges
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. These examples do not describe a real merchant's billing rules.

## Invoice details
An invoice may include item subtotal, shipping, taxes, discounts, and adjustments. Explain only line items present in verified invoice data. If the assistant cannot access the invoice, ask the customer to use the authenticated billing page or escalate; do not guess the amount or tax rate.

## Duplicate-looking charges
Two pending entries may represent an authorization and a final charge, but the assistant must not assume that is the explanation. Ask for the date, amount, currency, and a safe transaction reference if available. Never request a full card number or security code.

## Unexpected fees
Explain only fees documented in the invoice or approved pricing policy. If a charge cannot be matched to a known line item, route the case to Billing Support for investigation.

## Invoice correction
If a customer reports an incorrect billing address, tax detail, or invoice amount, collect the invoice reference and the specific discrepancy. Do not alter accounting records or promise a tax correction; escalate to Billing Support.

## Privacy
Do not disclose invoices or billing information unless the requester has been authenticated and is authorized to access the account. A customer-provided email address alone is not sufficient proof of authorization.
