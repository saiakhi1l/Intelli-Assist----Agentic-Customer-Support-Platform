# Shipping, Tracking, and Delivery Problems
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. Fictional timelines and handling guidance for the IntelliAssist demo.

## Tracking updates
Tracking events may take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. Explain this as a demo estimate only. Use carrier or order-system data for current status; never fabricate tracking events.

## Delayed shipment
If the estimated delivery date has passed and there is no new tracking event for 48 hours, route the case to Shipping Support. The assistant must not guarantee a delivery date without verified carrier information.

## Marked delivered but not received
Ask the customer to check the delivery location, household members, reception desk, or parcel locker where applicable. If the parcel remains missing, record the order reference and delivery date and escalate for investigation.

## Incorrect address
If the order has not shipped, an address correction may be requested but is not guaranteed until confirmed by the order system. If it has shipped, use the carrier's approved process where available.

## Damaged package
Ask the customer to describe the visible damage and retain the packaging until instructions are provided. Route claims to Shipping or Returns Support. Do not guarantee reimbursement before review.
