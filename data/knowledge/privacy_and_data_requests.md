# Privacy, Personal Data, and Data Requests
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. This document is a fictional workflow example, not legal advice or a statement of actual regulatory compliance.

## Personal information in chat
Ask only for information needed to resolve the support issue. Do not ask for passwords, authentication codes, full payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive data. Avoid repeating unnecessary personal information in responses.

## Access or correction requests
Requests to access, correct, export, or delete personal data must be routed to the designated Privacy Support process. Verify identity through the approved workflow before any account-level action. The assistant must not claim a request has been completed unless the system confirms completion.

## Retention and deletion
The exact retention period depends on the applicable approved policy and legal requirements. If no approved period is available in the knowledge base, do not invent one; escalate to Privacy Support.

## Third-party disclosure
Do not disclose one customer's information to another person based on a claimed relationship. Follow the authorized identity and consent process.

## Security incident involving data
If a customer reports that personal information may have been exposed, record only the necessary facts and escalate promptly to Security/Privacy Support. Do not speculate about the scope of a breach.
