# Refund Eligibility and Processing
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. All timelines and conditions below are fictional test policies.

## Standard refund window
A customer may request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery for an eligible purchase. The order must be identifiable and the item must meet the applicable return conditions. Requests after 30 days are not automatically eligible under this demo policy.

## Requests after the window
For a request made more than 30 days after delivery, do not promise a refund. Explain that the standard window has passed and offer review by Support if the customer describes exceptional circumstances. A request after 90 days is outside the standard policy and requires human review; the assistant must not invent an exception.

## Damaged or defective item
Ask for the order reference and a concise description of the damage. If permitted by the support workflow, request a photo through the approved secure channel. Do not ask the customer to post private information publicly. Route the case to Returns Support for eligibility and remedy review.

## Refund processing time
Once a refund is approved, the demo processing estimate is 5–10 business days, depending on the payment provider. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. The assistant must distinguish approval time from the time funds appear in the customer's account.

## Non-refundable cases
Digital services already consumed and clearly marked final-sale items are not refundable under this synthetic standard policy, except where applicable law or a human-reviewed exception requires otherwise. If the policy or purchase details do not establish eligibility, abstain from promising an outcome.
