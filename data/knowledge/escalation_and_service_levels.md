# Support Escalation, Priorities, and Service Expectations
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. Priority definitions and response targets are illustrative, not real service commitments.

## Immediate/high-priority escalation
Escalate promptly for reported account takeover, unauthorized charges, suspected exposure of personal data, credible safety risks, or active security threats. Do not keep the customer in an automated troubleshooting loop when the incident needs specialist review.

## Standard escalation
Escalate cases that require account verification, billing adjustments, warranty decisions, policy exceptions, or actions unavailable to the assistant. Provide a concise summary and the information already collected so the customer does not need to repeat everything.

## Illustrative service targets
For this demo only, high-priority cases target an initial human review within 4 business hours, and standard cases within 2 business days. These are example targets, not guarantees. Never state that a ticket has been assigned or reviewed unless the ticket system confirms it.

## Ticket summaries
A useful ticket includes issue category, customer-stated impact, relevant order or transaction reference where safe, steps already tried, and reason for escalation. Exclude passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers, and unnecessary personal data.

## When to abstain
If the knowledge base does not establish the answer, say that you cannot confirm the policy from the available information and offer an appropriate human handoff. Do not fabricate policy details, eligibility, dates, or completed actions.
