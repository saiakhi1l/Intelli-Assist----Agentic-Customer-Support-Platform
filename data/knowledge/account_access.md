# Account Access and Recovery
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. These rules are fictional examples for the IntelliAssist learning project and are not an actual company's policies.

## Password reset
Customers can request a password-reset link from the sign-in page using the email address registered to the account. The link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. If it expires, request a new link rather than reusing the old one.

## Reset email not received
Ask the customer to check spam/junk folders and confirm they entered the correct registered email address. Allow up to 5 minutes for delivery. Do not ask the customer to disclose their password or a reset token in chat. If the email still does not arrive, escalate to Account Support.

## Locked account
After 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes. A password reset may be used while waiting. Support agents must not bypass identity verification or manually disclose credentials.

## Email address changes
Changing the registered email requires successful sign-in and a verification link sent to the new address. If the customer cannot access the old account or cannot sign in, refer the case to Account Support for identity verification.

## Safe handling
Never ask for a full password, one-time passcode, recovery code, or complete payment-card number. Support staff must not change account ownership based only on a chat message.
