# Subscriptions, Renewals, and Plan Changes
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. These are fictional subscription rules for evaluation only.

## Renewal date
The renewal date and price must be verified from the customer's authenticated subscription record. Do not infer a renewal date from a generic plan description.

## Cancelling a subscription
A customer may request cancellation through the account's subscription settings. Cancellation is complete only when the system confirms it. Under this demo policy, cancellation stops the next renewal but does not automatically refund a prior payment.

## Plan changes
Upgrades or downgrades may take effect immediately or at the next billing cycle depending on the plan. Verify the plan-specific terms before stating the effective date or prorated amount.

## Trial conversion
If a trial is due to convert to a paid subscription, use the verified plan record to explain the date and amount. If those details are unavailable, direct the customer to the subscription page or Billing Support.

## Refund questions
Subscription refunds are governed by the standard refund policy and any verified plan-specific terms. Do not invent a cancellation exception or guarantee a refund without evidence.
