# Account Profile, Notifications, and Preferences
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. Fictional account-management guidance for the IntelliAssist demo.

## Profile updates
Customers should update profile details from the authenticated account settings page where that feature is available. If a change affects account ownership or security, route it through the approved verification process.

## Notification settings
Email or in-app notification preferences can be changed in settings if the option is available. Security-critical messages may be handled differently from marketing preferences. Do not promise that disabling a preference stops legally or operationally required messages.

## Missing notifications
Ask the customer to verify the destination address, spam/junk folder, and notification preferences. If the event itself is not visible in the account system, do not claim the notification was sent; escalate if necessary.

## Deactivating an account
Account deactivation or deletion requests must follow the approved identity-verification and privacy workflow. Explain potential loss of access only when supported by the verified account terms. Do not state that data has been deleted until the relevant system confirms it.

## Unauthorized profile changes
Treat an unexpected email, phone, or security-setting change as a possible security incident and escalate promptly. Do not reveal the previous value of a sensitive field to an unverified requester.
