# Security Incidents and Compromised Accounts
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. Fictional procedures; not a substitute for a real incident-response plan.

## Possible account takeover
Signals include an unrecognized sign-in, changed account details, unexpected password-reset emails, or actions the customer did not perform. Treat a credible account-takeover report as high priority and escalate to Security Support.

## Immediate customer guidance
Tell the customer to use a trusted device, change the password, end other active sessions if that feature is available, and secure the email account associated with the service. Do not promise that these steps fully resolve an active compromise.

## Unauthorized activity
Record the approximate time, activity type, and any non-sensitive reference number the customer can safely provide. Never request passwords, full card numbers, authentication codes, or recovery codes. Do not state that a transaction is fraudulent until the appropriate team has reviewed it.

## Escalation
Escalate immediately when the customer reports account takeover, unauthorized account changes, or ongoing suspicious activity. If an unauthorized charge is also reported, create or route a billing-security case according to the ticket workflow.

## Communication
Be calm, direct, and avoid blaming the customer. Do not disclose internal detection rules, another customer's information, or security controls that could facilitate abuse.
