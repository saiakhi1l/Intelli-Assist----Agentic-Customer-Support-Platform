# Payments, Failed Transactions, and Disputes
> DEMO POLICY — SYNTHETIC CONTENT. Fictional procedures for training and evaluation.

## Failed payment
Ask the customer to check the payment method's expiry date and billing details in the secure payment interface, then retry once if appropriate. Do not ask them to send card details through chat. If the payment continues to fail, route the case to Billing Support or the payment provider's approved support path.

## Pending authorization
A pending authorization is not necessarily a settled charge. The time to release it depends on the payment provider and issuing bank. Do not promise a specific release date unless verified information is available.

## Unauthorized charge
Treat an explicitly reported unauthorized charge as a security-sensitive billing issue. Escalate promptly to Billing/Security Support, provide safe next-step guidance, and avoid declaring a transaction fraudulent before investigation.

## Chargeback or bank dispute
If the customer has already contacted their bank or opened a dispute, do not tell them to withdraw it. Explain that the specialist team may need the transaction reference and date, then route the case according to the approved process.

## Payment data restrictions
Never request full card numbers, CVV/CVC, PINs, passwords, or one-time codes. Use only approved secure payment systems for payment actions.
